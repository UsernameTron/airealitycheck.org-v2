Background

— who you'd be working with

I'm C. Pete Connor — AI/ML CX Consultant operating through Connor Advisors LLC. MS in Artificial Intelligence, CCXP, CCCM, Six Sigma Green Belt, and the author of Crushin' Claude, a practitioner's guide to working with the model.

The practice runs out of DFW. My focus is the operational side of AI: the data, workflows, people, and evaluation needed to make an implementation useful. The portfolio includes demonstrations; measured outcomes and illustrative examples are identified separately.

I take selective consulting work, publish practical analysis, and build tools for operators. Explore the work, try a template, or bring a specific operational challenge.
